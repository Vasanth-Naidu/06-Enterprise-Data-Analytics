* **Domain:** Enterprise Data Analytics, Business Intelligence (BI) Engineering, Automated ETL/ELT Pipelines, Risk & Control Dashboards, C-Suite Telemetry
* **Company Context:** Cross-Company Leadership (JPMorgan Chase, Dell Technologies, iGate & GE Capital)
* **Role:** Lead Data Analytics Architect & Product/Project Manager (PM)
* **Core Value Delivered:** Hybrid execution driving data analytics initiatives as both a hands-on developer (custom tools, SQL pipelines, Tableau views) and a Product/Project Manager (directing end-to-end delivery of complex governance telemetry systems like **iTrack** and enterprise CCOR dashboards). Bridged Jira, SharePoint, SQL mirror databases, and Information Security (IS) Control repositories into unified, automated visual dashboards—eliminating manual reporting NVA, enforcing risk coverage, and accelerating C-suite decision-making.

---

## 1. Operational Challenge & Scope:

* **Fragmented Operational & Risk Data:** Operational health metrics, project pipelines, and compliance audit records were trapped in disconnected systems (Jira, SharePoint lists, SQL databases, IS Control repositories), preventing unified risk oversight.
* **Manual Data Cleansing & High Latency:** Senior risk leads and analysts spent hours manually extracting, cross-referencing, and normalizing data across platforms, creating reporting delays and potential audit blind spots.
* **Firmwide Compliance & Audit Tracking Gaps:** Ensuring compliance across all enterprise Legal Entities required continuous monitoring of process risk levels (High/Medium/Low) against mandatory minimum audit thresholds.
* **Lack of Single-Source-of-Truth Telemetry:** Leadership lacked dynamic, automated dashboards to track real-time Intelligent Automation (IA) project roadmaps alongside firmwide IS control registration schedules.

---

## 2. Enterprise Data Architecture & BI Pipeline Framework:

1. **Jira, SharePoint & IS Controls IA Alignment Engine (JPMC CCOR - PM Lead)** — Architected and built a 4-in-1 master analytics suite for the Intelligent Automation (IA) team. Extracted Jira ticket data, bounced it against the SharePoint Book of Work (updating live statuses and timelines), and cross-referenced it with the Firmwide IS Control Standards repository to track registered assets, eligible/unregistered tools, and target compliance dates.
2. **Enterprise Legal Entity Risk & Control Coverage Dashboard (JPMC CCOR - PM Lead)** — Directed as PM the build of a real-time, firmwide compliance and operational risk dashboard. Automatically mapped all processes to risk tiers (High/Medium/Low), established mandatory minimum audit frequency controls, and tracked completed vs. pending audits. Interfaced directly via daily automated SQL mirror DB connections into Tableau, serving everyone from entry-level auditors to C-suite leadership.
3. **iTrack Operational Telemetry System (Wholesale Payment Ops - PM Lead)** — Acted as PM overseeing the architecture and floor deployment of **iTrack**, a proprietary tracking and health metrics system designed to log floor transaction volumes, SLA adherence, and agent productivity across operational teams in real time.
4. **Automated ETL & Mirror Database Pipelines** — Engineered automated data pipeline jobs using SQL queries and Alteryx workflows to extract, transform, and load raw data from mirror databases into centralized Tableau data sources.
5. **Data Governance, Access Control & Audit Readiness** — Standardized KPI definitions, role-based database access, and automated daily refresh schedules to ensure data integrity and full audit readiness.

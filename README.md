# Portfolio Module 06: Enterprise Data Analytics, BI Pipelines & Executive Health Dashboard Architecture

## Executive Summary:
* **Domain:** Enterprise Data Analytics, Business Intelligence (BI) Engineering, Automated ETL/ELT Pipelines, Risk & Control Dashboards, C-Suite Telemetry[cite: 5]
* **Company Context:** Cross-Company Leadership (JPMorgan Chase — Wholesale Payments Ops & CCOR, Dell Technologies, iGate & GE Capital)[cite: 5]
* **Role:** Lead Data Analytics Architect & Product/Project Manager (PM)[cite: 5]
* **Core Value Delivered:** Hybrid execution driving data analytics initiatives as both a hands-on developer (custom tools, SQL pipelines, Tableau views) and a Product/Project Manager (directing end-to-end delivery of governance telemetry systems like **iTrack**, 800+ RPA IS Control compliance trackers, and enterprise CCOR dashboards)[cite: 5]. Bridged Jira, SharePoint, SQL mirror databases, and Information Security (IS) Control repositories into unified, automated visual dashboards—eliminating manual reporting NVA, enforcing risk coverage, achieving 100% audit readiness, and accelerating C-suite decision-making[cite: 5].

---

## 1. Operational Challenge & Scope:

* **Unregistered & Governance-Risk Automation Assets (800+ RPA Solutions):** Over 800+ User-Developed Tools (UDTs) and RPA solutions across Wholesale Payments Operations required urgent identification, registration, and technical/operational documentation compliance under Firmwide IS Control Standards within a strict 9-month timeframe.
* **Frontline Bandwidth & Audit Gap Friction:** Operational RPA tool owners lacked the bandwidth, technical documentation, and specialized OCC audit knowledge required to complete complex IS control filings independently.
* **Fragmented Operational & Risk Data:** Operational health metrics, project pipelines, and compliance audit records were trapped in disconnected systems (Jira, SharePoint lists, SQL databases, IS Control repositories), preventing unified risk oversight.
* **Real-Time Floor Visibility Deficit:** Operations managers lacked real-time telemetry into production volumes, work-item aging, individual employee productivity, and historical volume trends, hindering proactive SLA management.
* **Firmwide Compliance & Audit Tracking Gaps:** Ensuring compliance across all enterprise Legal Entities required continuous monitoring of process risk levels (High/Medium/Low) against mandatory minimum audit thresholds.
* **Manual Data Cleansing & High Latency:** Senior risk leads and analysts spent hours manually extracting, cross-referencing, and normalizing data across platforms, creating reporting delays and potential audit blind spots.

---

## 2. Enterprise Data Architecture & BI Pipeline Framework:

1. **800+ RPA Solution Firmwide IS Control Compliance Engine (JPMC Wholesale Payments — 9-Month Sprint Lead)** — Led the 2-phase timebound governance initiative across 800+ RPA/UDT solutions. **Phase 1** identified unregistered floor tools, evaluated eligibility/dormancy, and enforced central repository registration. **Phase 2** directly hand-held busy tool owners through the technical and operational documentation process—applying SDLC checklists to execute **Retroactive Documentation** and **Post Facto Ratification** for legacy assets to guarantee 100% OCC audit adherence. Built a centralized Tableau compliance tracker providing real-time visibility across operations, technology, and senior leadership.
2. **iTrack Real-Time Operational Telemetry Engine (Wholesale Payment Ops - PM Lead)** — Spearheaded as PM the design and deployment of **iTrack**, a near real-time operational dashboard tracking transaction volumes, work-item aging queues, employee productivity metrics, and volume trend analytics to provide single-source-of-truth floor visibility.
3. **Jira, SharePoint & IS Controls IA Alignment Engine (JPMC CCOR - PM Lead)** — Architected and built a 4-in-1 master analytics suite for the Intelligent Automation (IA) team. Extracted Jira ticket data, bounced it against the SharePoint 'Book of Work' (updating live statuses and timelines), and cross-referenced it with the Firmwide IS Control Standards repository to track registered assets, eligible/unregistered tools, and target compliance dates.
4. **Enterprise Legal Entity Risk & Control Coverage Dashboard (JPMC CCOR - PM Lead)** — Directed as PM the build of a real-time, firmwide compliance and operational risk dashboard. Automatically mapped all processes to risk tiers (High/Medium/Low), established mandatory minimum audit frequency controls, and tracked completed, scheduled and pending audits. Interfaced directly via daily automated SQL mirror DB connections into Tableau, serving everyone from entry-level auditors to C-suite leadership.
5. **Automated ETL/ELT & Mirror Database Pipelines** — Engineered automated data pipeline jobs using SQL queries, Alteryx workflows and Xceptor to extract, transform, and load raw data from mirror databases into centralized Tableau data sources.
6. **Data Governance, Access Control & Audit Readiness** — Standardized KPI definitions, role-based database access, and automated daily refresh schedules to ensure data integrity and full audit readiness.

## 3. Core Analytics & Technical Toolkit Built:

* **800+ RPA IS Control Compliance Tracker:** Timebound Tableau analytics suite monitoring 2-phase compliance, tool eligibility, dormancy status, retroactive documentation milestones, and technical/operational certification for 800+ RPA solutions.
* **iTrack Operational Telemetry Platform:** PM-led custom metrics system tracking near real-time transaction volumes, aging queues, employee productivity, and volume trend rollups.
* **IA Master Compliance & Delivery Dashboard (4-in-1 Suite):** Unified analytics engine combining Jira sprint tracking, SharePoint Book of Work, Firmwide IS Control Standards repository, and a consolidated master execution view.
* **Legal Entity Risk & Audit Coverage Dashboard:** Enterprise-wide Tableau dashboard fed by daily SQL mirror database refreshes, tracking High/Medium/Low risk process audit compliance.
* **SQL & Alteryx Data Pipeline Suite:** Library of optimized SQL queries, mirror DB connectors, and Alteryx ETL/ELT workflows automating cross-platform data joins and metric rollups.
